# Tallyho brand guide (excerpt)

Tallyho is a EUR 12/month invoicing app for Dutch freelancers (zzp'ers).

## Voice
- Friendly, plain and direct. Talk to the reader as "you". Never "users" or "customers" in copy addressed to them.
- Confident, not loud: at most one exclamation mark per piece.
- Never use "simply", "just" or "easy" about a task; it may not be easy for the reader.

## Terminology
- Product name is always "Tallyho" (never TallyHo, TALLYHO, Tally-ho).
- Say "invoice", never "bill".
- Dutch tax terms in lowercase: "btw", "kvk-nummer".

## Claims and legal
- No superlatives we cannot prove ("best", "#1", "fastest").
- Any time-saving or money figure needs a source in a footnote.
- Anything about tax filing or penalties must end with: "Check with your accountant for your situation."
- Never name or disparage competitors in customer-facing copy.

## Messaging pillars
1. Get paid faster (payment links on every invoice).
2. Tax-ready all year (btw overview per quarter).
3. Made for zzp'ers (Dutch-first, iDEAL, kvk-nummer on every invoice).
