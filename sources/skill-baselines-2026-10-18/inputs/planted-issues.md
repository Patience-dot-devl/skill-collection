# Planted brand-guide violations (grader answer key)

## draft-email.md (11)
E1. Product name written "TallyHo" (subject, body, sign-off) instead of "Tallyho".
E2. "#1 invoicing app" - unprovable superlative.
E3. More than one exclamation mark (subject, "ready!", "today!").
E4. "users" used for the reader ("users like you", "Our users").
E5. "bill" used instead of "invoice" (twice).
E6. "Simply" about a task.
E7. "BTW" in uppercase instead of "btw".
E8. Tax-filing claim ("file your BTW return automatically") without the required line "Check with your accountant for your situation."
E9. "never pay a late penalty again" - absolute guarantee about tax penalties; unprovable and legally risky.
E10. "save 10 hours every month" - time-saving figure without a footnoted source.
E11. "Simply connect your bank" - promises a bank connection; if the reader also has the competitive context, Tallyho has no bank feed (factual risk). Credit if flagged as unverified product claim.
