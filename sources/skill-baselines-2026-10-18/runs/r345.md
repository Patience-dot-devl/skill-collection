# Brand review: Tallyho launch email

Reviewed file: `<eval-root>/inputs/draft-email.md`
Brand guide used: `<eval-root>/inputs/tallyho-brand-guide.md`

## Assumptions

- The brand guide is the excerpt supplied in the inputs folder. It covers voice, terminology, claims and legal, and messaging pillars. It does not cover grammar rules such as the Oxford comma or date formats, so those were not checked.
- The guide was provided, so the generic review was not used.
- The email is an English-language launch email for Dutch freelancers (zzp'ers), as the guide describes the audience.
- I did not verify the product claims (bank connection, free trial, time savings) against the product or any source. The guide gives no evidence for them, so they are flagged for confirmation.
- No connectors or web search were needed. This is not a legal review.

## Summary

- Overall assessment: the draft does not yet meet the brand standard and should not ship as written. It breaks several hard rules: a superlative, an unsupported guarantee about tax penalties, a missing accountant disclaimer, unsourced figures, and the banned word "simply". It also misspells the product name in every mention.
- Biggest strengths: the core benefits line up with the messaging pillars. The email mentions iDEAL (pillar 3), it uses "btw" in one place, and the sign-off is clear and short.
- Most important improvements: remove "#1" and the penalty guarantee, rewrite the BTW bullet so it matches pillar 2 (a btw overview per quarter, not automatic filing) and add the required accountant sentence, fix the product name, and cut the exclamation marks to one.

## Legal and compliance flags

- Unsubstantiated superlative: "#1 invoicing app" (`draft-email.md:1`). Remove it, or back it with a published, verifiable source before use.
- Guarantee and tax-filing claim: "never pay a late penalty again" and automatic BTW filing (`draft-email.md:10`). This is a tax and penalty claim with no support. Remove it. Add "Check with your accountant for your situation." to any tax-related copy.
- Unsourced figures: "30 seconds" (`draft-email.md:8`) and "10 hours every month" (`draft-email.md:12`). Each needs a footnote source (guide line 17).
- Trial offer: "free trial" (`draft-email.md:12`). Confirm the terms and any disclosures needed.
- Feature claim: "Connect your bank" (`draft-email.md:5`). Confirm it is accurate.
