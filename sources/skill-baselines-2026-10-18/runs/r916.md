# Brand review: draft landing page against the Tallyho brand guide

Files reviewed: `draft-landing.md` (the copy) against `tallyho-brand-guide.md` (the guide). Line references below point to `draft-landing.md` unless stated otherwise.

Assumptions: the brand guide excerpt is the complete set of rules for this review. "Customers" on line 7 means the reader's own clients, not the reader. I could not check any of the figures in the draft, so I treat them all as unsourced.

## Summary

**Overall:** the draft does not meet the brand guide yet. In 10 lines it breaks most of the guide's rules: it uses a superlative, names and criticises a competitor, guarantees a tax result, uses an unsourced figure, uses banned words, and has two terminology errors and a double exclamation mark.

**Strengths:** the structure is tight and easy to scan. Two of the three messaging pillars are already present: payment links (pillar 1, line 7) and the quarterly btw overview (pillar 2, line 8).

**Most important fixes:** remove the "best" claim and the Moneybird comparison (line 3), and remove the filing guarantee (line 8). That sentence must also end with the required accountant line. Either source the "2x faster" figure (line 7) or cut it.

## Legal and compliance flags

1. **Tax guarantee (line 8).** "Guaranteed to be correct" promises a filing result that depends on what the user enters, and it lacks the mandatory accountant line. Remove the guarantee and add the line. A compliance owner should approve the final wording.
2. **Competitor named and disparaged (line 3).** Calling Moneybird "clunky" is a comparative and disparaging claim that the competitor could challenge, and the brand guide forbids it. Delete it.
3. **Unprovable superlative (line 3).** "The best" is an unsubstantiated claim. Remove it.
4. **Unsourced performance figure (line 7).** "2x faster" needs a footnoted source with its baseline and method, or it must be removed.
5. **User count (line 10).** Verify "5,000+" before publishing, and footnote the source and date so the claim stays accurate over time.
