# Brand review: Tallyho launch email

**Verdict: not ready to ship.** The draft (`draft-email.md`) breaks almost every section of the brand guide (`tallyho-brand-guide.md`). Two of the problems are legal risks and must be fixed before it goes out: a tax-filing and penalty promise with no disclaimer, and figures with no source. I did not edit the draft. A corrected version is at the end of this review.

## Blocking issues (legal and claims)

1. **draft-email.md:10. The product does not file tax returns, and the line promises no penalties.** "Let TallyHo file your BTW return automatically, so you'll never pay a late penalty again." The messaging pillar is a "btw overview per quarter" (brand guide:23), not automatic filing. This line overclaims a feature and guarantees a tax outcome. Any copy about tax filing or penalties must end with "Check with your accountant for your situation." (brand guide:18), and the draft does not include it. Fix: describe the quarterly btw overview, drop the penalty promise, and add the required line.
2. **draft-email.md:1. "#1 invoicing app" is a superlative we cannot prove** (brand guide:16).
3. **draft-email.md:12. "save 10 hours every month" has no source**, and **draft-email.md:8. "in 30 seconds"** is also a time-saving figure without one. Both need a sourced footnote (brand guide:17). If there is no source, remove them.
4. **draft-email.md:5. "Simply connect your bank".** "Simply" is banned (brand guide:8). Connecting a bank is also not one of the three pillars (brand guide:21-24). Confirm with product that this feature exists before it appears in any copy.

## Terminology

5. **Product name.** The draft writes "TallyHo" five times: draft-email.md:1, 5, 7, 10 and 14. It must always be "Tallyho" (brand guide:11).
6. **"bill" instead of "invoice"** at draft-email.md:5 and draft-email.md:8 (brand guide:12).
7. **"BTW" should be lowercase "btw"** at draft-email.md:10 (brand guide:13).

## Voice

8. **"users"** at draft-email.md:5 ("for users like you") and draft-email.md:12 ("Our users"). Talk to the reader as "you" (brand guide:6).
9. **Three exclamation marks** at draft-email.md:1, 5 and 12. The limit is one per piece (brand guide:7). Keep only the one on the call to action.

## Missed opportunities (not violations)

- The draft leaves out pillar 1, "get paid faster with payment links on every invoice" (brand guide:22), and the kvk-nummer on every invoice from pillar 3 (brand guide:24). Both are strong launch messages.
- The audience is Dutch zzp'ers (brand guide:3). Saying "zzp'ers" instead of only "freelancers" fits "Made for zzp'ers".
- The price (EUR 12/month, brand guide:3) is a clear, honest selling point and could go next to the call to action.
- "sending a bill from Word" (draft-email.md:5) mentions a generic tool, not an invoicing competitor, so it does not break brand guide:19. It is fine to keep once "bill" becomes "invoice".

## Suggested corrected version

> **Subject:** Tallyho is here: invoicing made for zzp'ers
>
> Hi there,
>
> Tired of building invoices in Word? We made Tallyho for you.
>
> With Tallyho you can:
> - Send a professional invoice with your kvk-nummer on it
> - Add a payment link to every invoice, so you get paid faster
> - Let clients pay you with iDEAL
> - See your btw for each quarter, so you are ready when it is time to file
>
> Tallyho costs EUR 12 a month. Start your free trial today!
>
> Check with your accountant for your situation.
>
> The Tallyho team

## Assumptions

- A free trial exists, because the draft offers one. The brand guide does not mention it, so confirm it before sending.
- No sources were provided for "10 hours" or "30 seconds", so I removed both. To put them back, add a footnote with the source.
- I read "must end with" (brand guide:18) as the email body ending with the accountant line, so it sits just before the sign-off.
