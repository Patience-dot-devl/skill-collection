# Brand review: Tallyho launch email

References are to `draft-email.md` (the email) and `tallyho-brand-guide.md` (the guide).

**Assumptions:** The email goes to Dutch freelancers (zzp'ers), who may be prospects or trial sign-ups. I treated the free trial and the bank connection as real features, but neither appears in the guide, so please confirm both before sending. I did not edit the source file. The proposed changes are below.

## Summary

- **Overall assessment:** The email is not ready to send. It is short and well structured, and it mentions iDEAL, which supports the "Made for zzp'ers" pillar. However, it breaks almost every rule in the guide. The product name is wrong every time it appears. It uses three banned words or terms ("users", "bill", "simply") and has three exclamation marks where the limit is one. It also makes four claims that are compliance risks: "#1", "30 seconds", "10 hours" and automatic tax filing with no late penalties.
- **Biggest strength:** The structure is clear: a hook, three benefit bullets, a figure and a call to action. iDEAL is a good detail for this audience.
- **Most important fixes:** Remove or source the claims, especially the claim that Tallyho files btw returns. The guide only promises a quarterly btw overview, so that claim may be untrue as well as unsupported. After that, fix the product name and the terminology throughout.

## Revised sections

**1. Tax claim (High)**
- Before: "Let TallyHo file your BTW return automatically, so you'll never pay a late penalty again"
- After: "See your btw for every quarter in one overview, ready for your return"
- Add at the end of the email: "Check with your accountant for your situation."

**2. Subject line (High)**
- Before: "TallyHo is here - the #1 invoicing app for freelancers!"
- After: "Tallyho is here: invoicing made for zzp'ers"

**3. Time-saving claims (High)**
- Before: "Our users save 10 hours every month. Start your free trial today!"
- After: "Freelancers using Tallyho save around 10 hours a month.¹ Start your free trial today!"
- Footnote: "¹ Source: [survey name, date, sample size]." If there is no source, cut the sentence and keep only the call to action.
- Before: "Send a professional bill in 30 seconds"
- After: "Send a professional invoice, with your kvk-nummer already on it, in a few clicks" (or keep "30 seconds" and add a footnote with the source)

**4. Opening paragraph (Medium: name, "users", "bill", "simply", exclamation mark)**
- Before: "We built TallyHo for users like you who are tired of sending a bill from Word. Simply connect your bank and you're ready!"
- After: "We built Tallyho for freelancers like you who are tired of making invoices in a word processor. Connect your bank and you're ready to send your first invoice."

## Legal and compliance flags

1. **Possible false feature claim and guarantee** (draft-email.md:10). The draft says Tallyho files the btw return automatically and promises no late penalties. Product and legal teams should confirm what Tallyho actually does. Do not guarantee any tax outcome. The accountant line is required (guide:18).
2. **Unsubstantiated superlative** (draft-email.md:1). "#1" cannot be proved and is banned by name in guide:16. Remove it.
3. **Unsourced figures** (draft-email.md:8, :12). "30 seconds" and "10 hours every month" each need a source footnote (guide:17). The "10 hours" figure is presented as a result for all customers, so it needs a real study behind it, or it should be removed.
4. **Third-party product name** (draft-email.md:5). "Word" is a Microsoft trademark. The risk is low, but a generic phrase avoids the question entirely.
