# Competitive Brief: Tallyho vs Moneybird and e-Boekhouden

**Research date:** 2026-10-18. All competitor prices and features come from the competitors' public websites and review pages on this date. Prices in this market changed this year, so check them again before you put them on a battlecard.

**Assumptions** (no clarifying questions were asked):

- Tallyho is an invoicing app, not a full bookkeeping package. It has no general ledger and does not file the btw-aangifte for the user. The "quarterly btw overview" is a summary the freelancer or their accountant uses to file.
- EUR 12/month is the list price with no first-year discount. It is unclear whether this includes 21% btw. Moneybird and e-Boekhouden do not say clearly on their pricing pages either.
- The iDEAL payment links run through a payment service provider such as Mollie or Stripe. Whether Tallyho passes on a fee per transaction is unknown. This matters for the price comparison (see section 3).
- The buyer is a Dutch sole trader (zzp'er / eenmanszaak) who usually has no employees.

---

## 1. Executive summary

The premise that Tallyho is "the cheaper option" holds against only one of the two competitors. **e-Boekhouden is cheaper than Tallyho.** Its ZZP plan costs EUR 9.95/month, or EUR 4.98/month for the first 12 months, and new businesses get it free for 15 months. It also includes an automatic bank connection. Against Moneybird, Tallyho is EUR 3/month cheaper than the Start plan (EUR 15). Moneybird Start, however, *also* has no bank feed since Moneybird changed its plans in July 2026. Moneybird now sells the bank feed only in Groei (EUR 29), so Tallyho is EUR 17/month cheaper than the cheapest Moneybird plan that has one.

**Recommended position:** stop leading on price. Position Tallyho as the invoicing app that gets freelancers paid. Every invoice has a payment link, and the invoice marks itself as paid when the client pays, with no bank feed and no bookkeeping. This is a niche inside the category: "invoicing and getting paid" for freelancers who do not want an accounting package.

- **Biggest opportunity:** Moneybird's July 2026 repackaging. Its Trustpilot score is now 2.3/5, and recent reviews complain about price rises and about the bank feed moving to a more expensive plan. Many of these users want something simple and fairly priced.
- **Biggest threat:** e-Boekhouden. It is the obvious place for those same Moneybird users to go. It costs less than Tallyho, has a bank feed, claims more than 500,000 users and shows a 4.5 Trustpilot score on its homepage. In any deal where the buyer compares feature lists and prices, Tallyho loses to e-Boekhouden.

## 2. Competitor profiles

### 2.1 Moneybird

**Company overview**

- **What they do:** Online bookkeeping and invoicing for Dutch small businesses. It includes a general ledger, an electronic btw-aangifte, Peppol e-invoicing, an API and an optional business account (the Moneybird Betaalrekening, run on Adyen).
- **Target audience:** Freelancers up to small teams. The plans are labelled "for freelancers and starters" (Start), "for growing businesses" (Groei) and "no limits" (Compleet).
- **Stage:** Established; it has been around since 2008. Moneybird itself calls the July 2026 change the first revision of its plans since 2008.
- **Key recent development:** The July 2026 plan restructure. There are now four plans: Compact EUR 3, Start EUR 15, Groei EUR 29 and Compleet EUR 41 per month. Automatic bank connections are only in Groei and Compleet. Start allows a manual import of up to 20 bank transactions per month, and unused transactions expire. Existing customers get a 12-month transition discount.

**Messaging analysis**

- **Headline on the pricing page:** "Betaal alleen voor wat je nodig hebt" ("Pay only for what you need").
- **Core value proposition:** Complete, modern bookkeeping that you can scale up plan by plan.
- **Themes:** completeness (ledger, btw, Peppol, API), modular pricing, automation such as receipt scanning, and the integrated payment account.
- **Tone:** Clean, confident and product-led.
- **Problem they describe:** Bookkeeping admin takes time; Moneybird automates it.
- **Messaging vulnerability:** "Pay only for what you need" is now hard to believe for small users. Reviewers say they must buy Groei, with five users and five bank connections, just to get one bank feed back. One reviewer wrote on 2026-09-24: "Je betaalt dus vol voor vijf gebruikers en vijf koppelingen die je nooit gebruikt" ("So you pay in full for five users and five connections you never use").

**Product and pricing**

| Plan | Price/month | Bank feed | Sales invoices | Electronic btw return | iDEAL on invoices |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compact | EUR 3 | No (5 manual transactions) | 1 | No | Yes |
| Start | EUR 15 | No (20 manual transactions) | Unlimited | Yes | Yes |
| Groei | EUR 29 | Yes (5 accounts) | Unlimited | Yes | Yes |
| Compleet | EUR 41 | Yes (unlimited) | Unlimited | Yes | Yes |

iDEAL payments cost EUR 0.29 per transaction, and SEPA direct debit costs EUR 0.25. The trial lasts 60 days with full Compleet features.

**Strengths:** It is a full bookkeeping package, so the freelancer can do everything without an accountant. It has strong brand recognition among Dutch freelancers, iDEAL payment links on every plan, Peppol support, a large ecosystem and API, and a long trial. Onderneming.nl rated it 9/10 in July 2026.

**Weaknesses:** Customers are angry about the repackaging. The Trustpilot score is 2.3/5 from 252 reviews, and 34% of reviews give one star. Recurring complaints are price rises (one reviewer cites EUR 15 rising to EUR 25.41, and to EUR 35 next year), the loss of the bank feed on cheaper plans, no telephone support, a frustrating chatbot, and some reports of payouts being held back. Start's limit of 20 manual bank transactions per month is a friction point.

**Narrative:** The villain is admin chaos and the hero is the product. The transformation is "your bookkeeping is done". The stakes are tax deadlines and lost time.

**Positioning statement (reverse-engineered):** For Dutch entrepreneurs from freelancer to small team, Moneybird is the online bookkeeping platform that does all your admin in one place, because it combines ledger, invoicing, btw, payments and bank in one product.

### 2.2 e-Boekhouden.nl

**Company overview**

- **What they do:** Online bookkeeping and invoicing software that the Belastingdienst recognises, aimed squarely at price-sensitive Dutch entrepreneurs and their bookkeepers.
- **Target audience:** Starters and zzp'ers through to small and medium businesses. It claims a network of more than 5,000 bookkeepers.
- **Scale:** It claims more than 500,000 entrepreneurs have used it, and its homepage shows 4.5 on Trustpilot from 2,059 reviews.
- **Key recent development:** Aggressive acquisition offers. Starters get 15 months free from their KvK registration. New customers who activate within 30 days get 50% off for 12 months.

**Messaging analysis**

- **Headline:** "Boekhouden en factureren op een manier die jij begrijpt" ("Bookkeeping and invoicing in a way you understand"). The tagline is "Slim geautomatiseerd boekhouden en factureren" ("Smartly automated bookkeeping and invoicing").
- **Core value proposition:** Simple, affordable bookkeeping with personal support. It claims "the best price-quality ratio".
- **Themes:** ease of use ("ongekend eenvoudig", "incredibly easy"), price and free offers for starters, recognition by the Belastingdienst, a fast support centre, and automatic matching of payments to open invoices through bank, webshop and till connections.
- **Tone:** Friendly, practical and value-focused, with less polish than Moneybird.
- **Problem they describe:** Bookkeeping feels hard and expensive; e-Boekhouden makes it understandable and cheap.

**Product and pricing**

| Plan | Price/month | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Starter offer | Free for 15 months | All functions, from KvK registration |
| ZZP | EUR 4.98 for 12 months, then EUR 9.95 | Maximum 240 bookings per year; unlimited invoicing |
| Standaard | EUR 7.25, then EUR 14.50 | Unlimited bookings, **no invoicing** |
| Standaard + Facturen | EUR 12.00, then EUR 24.00 | Unlimited bookings and invoicing |

The trial lasts 14 days. The website lists automatic bank connections and a mobile app. A Rabobank manual describes a daily automatic import of transactions. **Not verified:** whether the bank feed is in every plan, and whether invoices carry a built-in iDEAL payment link. The site names Mollie as a partner but does not describe a payment link on invoices. Sales should test this in a trial account before claiming it as a gap.

**Strengths:** It is the cheapest full bookkeeping option, it has the bank feed, it has a large installed base, good reviews and accountant distribution, and its starter offer is very hard to beat on price.

**Weaknesses:** The product is broad and bookkeeping-first, so a freelancer who only wants to invoice has to learn ledger concepts. The ZZP plan is capped at 240 bookings per year, about 20 per month. A freelancer with many small transactions may hit the cap and be pushed to EUR 24/month. Exactly what counts as a "booking" is not confirmed. The prices for the first year are discounts, and the list prices are double. Getting paid by payment link is not a visible part of its pitch.

**Narrative:** The villain is complexity and cost. The hero is the entrepreneur, who "understands" their bookkeeping. The transformation runs from confusion to control at a low price.

**Positioning statement (reverse-engineered):** For cost-conscious Dutch entrepreneurs, e-Boekhouden is the bookkeeping and invoicing software that is easy to understand and cheap to run, because it is recognised by the Belastingdienst, comes with personal support and has entry pricing that is close to free.

| Dimension | Tallyho | Moneybird | e-Boekhouden |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary headline | (recommended) "Stuur een factuur. Krijg betaald." ("Send an invoice. Get paid.") | "Betaal alleen voor wat je nodig hebt" | "Boekhouden en factureren op een manier die jij begrijpt" |
| Category | Invoicing and getting paid | Online bookkeeping platform | Online bookkeeping and invoicing |
| Target buyer | Freelancer who invoices clients and leaves bookkeeping to an accountant or a simple quarterly routine | Freelancer to small team who wants all admin in one tool | Price-sensitive starters and zzp'ers, and their bookkeepers |
| Key differentiator | A payment link on every invoice by default; the invoice is marked paid automatically; a btw overview per quarter | Completeness and ecosystem | Price and simplicity |
| Bank feed | Not yet | Groei (EUR 29) and up | Yes (plan inclusion not verified) |
| Entry price for invoicing | EUR 12 | EUR 15 (Start) | EUR 9.95 (EUR 4.98 in year 1; free for 15 months for starters) |
| Tone | Plain, fast, freelancer-to-freelancer | Polished, product-led | Friendly, value-led |

**The real price comparison for sales:**

- **Against Moneybird Start (EUR 15 plus EUR 0.29 per iDEAL payment):** A freelancer who sends 15 invoices a month that are paid by iDEAL pays about EUR 19.35/month at Moneybird. If Tallyho does not charge a fee per payment, the gap is about EUR 7/month, or about EUR 88 a year. Confirm Tallyho's fee per transaction before using this number.
- **Against Moneybird Groei (EUR 29), for a buyer who wants a bank feed:** Tallyho is EUR 17/month cheaper but does not have the feature. This is a trade-off, not a win.
- **Against e-Boekhouden:** Tallyho costs more. Do not compete on price.

Tallyho should collect its own figures, such as median days to payment, to replace borrowed claims.

## 5. Opportunities

1. **Reframe the bank feed question.** For a freelancer the most important use of a bank feed is to know which invoices have been paid. A payment link that marks the invoice as paid answers that question directly. The bank feed then matters mostly for expenses and for bookkeeping, which is the accountant's job or a quarterly task. This turns "we don't have a bank feed" into "you don't need one to know who paid you".
2. **Moneybird's repackaging backlash.** Some Start users are paying more and have lost their bank feed. Tallyho does not win the ones who mainly want the bank feed back. It does win the ones whose pain is "I pay for a bookkeeping suite I barely use". Target that second group.
3. **An unclaimed angle.** Neither competitor leads with getting paid. Both lead with bookkeeping. "Getting paid" is an outcome; "bookkeeping" is a chore.
4. **The Wero transition.** In October 2026 all Dutch banks are connected to Wero, and the move from iDEAL to Wero should be complete by 31 December 2027 at the latest. Freelancers will have questions. Tallyho can own "your payment links keep working through the switch", provided that its payment provider confirms this.
5. **Freelancers with an accountant.** Freelancers who hand their administration to an accountant pay twice if they also buy a full bookkeeping package. Tallyho's quarterly btw overview, exported to the accountant, fits this group well. Partnerships with accountants can be a second channel, and e-Boekhouden's 5,000-bookkeeper network shows that the channel works.

## 6. Threats

1. **e-Boekhouden wins on both price and features** for buyers who compare checklists. It is the natural place for unhappy Moneybird users to go, and some Trustpilot reviewers say they are considering it.
2. **The bank feed is the most-discussed feature in this market right now** because of the Moneybird backlash. Buyers are primed to ask about it.
3. **Tallyho's iDEAL claim is not unique.** Moneybird has had iDEAL on every plan for a long time. The difference is in defaults and experience, not in having the feature, so sales must not claim "only we have iDEAL links".
4. **"iDEAL" as a word is ageing.** The co-brand is now "iDEAL | Wero", and messaging that relies on the iDEAL name alone will look dated by 2027.
5. **Moneybird may react** by easing the bank feed restriction or extending discounts, which would remove the backlash opportunity.

## 7. Recommended actions

### Where to position

**A niche within the category.** Tallyho is the invoicing app for Dutch freelancers who want to get paid, not to do bookkeeping.

> For Dutch freelancers who invoice clients and don't want to run a bookkeeping package, Tallyho is the invoicing app that gets your invoices paid sooner and keeps your btw ready each quarter, because every invoice carries an iDEAL | Wero payment link and marks itself paid the moment your client pays.

On a positioning map with "point solution vs platform" on one axis and "price" on the other, both competitors sit on the platform side. Tallyho should hold the "focused, fairly priced point solution" corner and not move into a feature race it cannot win yet.

### Quick wins (this week)

1. **Remove "cheaper" as the lead message** from the website and sales scripts. Use price only against Moneybird, and only with the full calculation.
2. **Change "iDEAL" to "iDEAL | Wero"** in all copy, and confirm with the payment provider how payment links behave during the migration.
3. **Give sales the battlecard below**, including a clear rule for when to walk away (see "Disqualify").
4. **Set up a trial account at e-Boekhouden** and check two facts: whether invoices get a payment link, and whether the bank feed is in the ZZP plan. Update the battlecard with the results.

### Strategic moves (this quarter)

5. **Measure and publish proof.** The key figure is the median number of days from invoice to payment for Tallyho users, compared with a baseline of bank transfer only. This turns the core claim into evidence.
6. **Publish an honest bank-feed roadmap.** Say that it is coming and say what it will do. Do not give a date that the product team has not committed to. In sales, this keeps buyers who need the feature soon instead of losing them.
7. **Build the "getting paid" and "Wero switch" content cluster**, plus a "Moneybird alternative for freelancers who only invoice" page. The page should compare honestly and include the bank feed difference.
8. **Test an accountant partnership**: export the quarterly btw overview in the format accountants want.

## Sales battlecard

**Last updated:** 2026-10-18. Win rate: not tracked yet, so start logging wins and losses by competitor now.

### What to say: the core pitch (30 seconds)

"Tallyho does one thing: it gets your invoices paid. Every invoice goes out with an iDEAL | Wero payment link, so your client pays in two taps. When they pay, the invoice marks itself as paid, so you always know who still owes you without checking your bank. At the end of each quarter your btw overview is ready to file or send to your accountant. It costs EUR 12 a month, with no bookkeeping package you won't use."

### Against Moneybird

- **Their pitch:** a complete bookkeeping platform in modular plans.
- **Be honest about their strengths:** full bookkeeping, a strong brand, iDEAL on every plan, Peppol and an API.
- **Our angle:** "If you mainly send invoices, you are paying for a bookkeeping suite. With Moneybird you now need Groei at EUR 29 a month to get a bank feed. With Tallyho you know who paid without one, for EUR 12."
- **Price:** EUR 12 against EUR 15 plus EUR 0.29 per iDEAL payment on Start. Confirm Tallyho's own fees before quoting.

### Against e-Boekhouden

- **Their pitch:** simple, cheap bookkeeping, recognised by the Belastingdienst.
- **Be honest about their strengths:** lower price, a bank feed, many users and good reviews.
- **Our angle:** "e-Boekhouden is a good bookkeeping package. Tallyho is not trying to be one. If you want to keep your own books, it is a strong choice. If you want to send invoices, get paid quickly and hand the quarter to your accountant, Tallyho is faster, and you never have to learn ledger accounts."
- **Never argue on price.** Move the conversation to time and getting paid.

### Objection handling

| If the prospect says... | Respond with... |
|---|---|
| "You don't have a bank feed." | "That's right, not yet, and it's on our roadmap. What do you use the bank feed for today? For most freelancers it's to see which invoices have been paid. In Tallyho that happens automatically: when your client pays through the link, the invoice is marked as paid. Your expenses and bank reconciliation stay with your accountant or your quarterly routine." |
| "Moneybird also has iDEAL on invoices." | "It does. The difference is that Tallyho is built around getting paid, not around bookkeeping. You don't need Moneybird's ledger, and you don't pay EUR 0.29 per payment [confirm]. If you need full bookkeeping, Moneybird is the better fit." |
| "e-Boekhouden is cheaper and has a bank feed." | "For full bookkeeping it's good value. Check two things: the ZZP plan is limited to 240 bookings a year, and the first-year price doubles afterwards. If all you need is invoicing and getting paid, ask yourself how much time a bookkeeping package costs you each month." |
| "I already use Moneybird." | "Did your plan change in July? Many freelancers now pay more for features they don't use. If you mainly send invoices, switching takes about [X minutes, confirm]." |
| "My accountant uses e-Boekhouden/Moneybird." | "Then send them Tallyho's quarterly btw overview, or ask them what format they want. Many accountants prefer clean input to a client's half-finished ledger." |

### Landmines to set (ask early)

- "How many of your invoices are paid late, and how much time do you spend chasing them?"
- "What do you actually use your bank feed for: knowing who paid you, or bookkeeping?"
- "Does your accountant do your books, or do you?"
- (Moneybird users) "Has your price changed since July?"
- (e-Boekhouden prospects) "About how many transactions go through your business account each year?" This raises the 240-booking cap on the ZZP plan. Confirm how e-Boekhouden counts bookings first.

### Landmines to defuse (what competitors will push)

- **"Can it do your btw-aangifte?"** Answer: "Tallyho prepares your quarterly btw overview, so you or your accountant can file it in minutes." Do not claim direct filing unless the product has it.
- **"Is it a real bookkeeping package?"** Answer: "No, on purpose. It's invoicing and getting paid. If you need a full ledger, we'll tell you."
- **"Will iDEAL still work?"** Answer: "Yes. iDEAL is becoming iDEAL | Wero, and our payment links move with it." Confirm with the payment provider first.

### Disqualify (walk away honestly)

Recommend a competitor when the prospect keeps their own full books without an accountant, needs a bank feed for expense reconciliation now, has employees or several users, or needs Peppol e-invoicing today. An honest "not yet" keeps the door open for when the bank feed ships.

### Win and loss themes (hypotheses to check with real deal data)

- **Likely wins:** freelancers with an accountant, those with many small B2C or SME clients who pay by link, and Moneybird Start users who are unhappy about the price rise.
- **Likely losses:** do-it-yourself bookkeepers, price shoppers comparing with e-Boekhouden's starter offer, and anyone who has decided that a bank feed is required.
