# Tallyho Launch Campaign Plan: "De laatste factuur uit Word"

## Assumptions

I made the following assumptions because the brief did not cover these points. Please correct any that are wrong, because several of them change the numbers.

- **The 6 weeks run from launch day.** That is Monday 2027-01-12 to Sunday 2027-02-22. Preparation happens before launch (from mid-November 2026). A lower-intensity "sustain phase" runs from 2027-02-23 to the goal date of 2027-03-31.
- **What counts as a "paid signup".** It is an account that has made its first successful payment by 2027-03-31 and has not been refunded. Monthly and annual plans each count once.
- **Trial model.** Tallyho offers a 14-day free trial with no card required. Trials started after about 2027-03-17 will mostly not convert before the deadline. So the real acquisition window for the goal ends in mid-March.
- **Product capabilities.** Tallyho produces invoices that meet Dutch requirements: sequential numbering, KvK and btw-id fields, 21%, 9% and 0% VAT rates, and reverse charge (btw verlegd). It also offers a payment link (iDEAL), automatic payment reminders, a quarterly btw overview, and import of customer lists from Excel or CSV. Every proof point below depends on these features. Product should confirm them before copy is written.
- **The waitlist.** It holds 4,000 people who opted in to hear about the launch. Their consent covers launch email. Uploading the list to Meta as a custom audience needs a separate check against the privacy policy they agreed to.
- **Team.** A small team: a founder or marketing lead, one product or design person, and freelancers for copy, design and video. Owners in the calendar are roles, not names.
- **Language.** All customer-facing material is in Dutch. This brief is in English.
- **The budget of EUR 15,000 is cash.** Discounts and free months are revenue you give up, not campaign cash, so they are shown separately.

## A note on the goal before the plan

1,500 paid accounts in about 11 weeks, with EUR 15,000 and no paid-ads history, is a stretch target. It is reachable, but only if the waitlist and word-of-mouth channels do most of the work. Paid ads cannot carry it. Here is a rough calculation for paid search. Assume a cost per click of EUR 2.50, 10% of clicks starting a trial, and 35% of trials converting to paid. That puts the cost of one paying customer at about EUR 70. At that price, EUR 15,000 spent entirely on ads would buy only about 210 customers.

So the plan is built around four engines, in order of importance:

1. Converting the waitlist with a founding-member offer.
2. Referrals from new customers.
3. Bookkeepers and accountants (boekhouders) recommending Tallyho to their zzp clients.
4. A high-intent hook for people who search for Word and Excel invoice templates.

Paid media is used to amplify and retarget, not as the main source.

| Scenario | Paid accounts by 2027-03-31 |
|---|---|
| Conservative (waitlist converts at 12%, partners slow to start) | about 1,000 |
| Plan target (channel targets below) | about 1,550 |

The decision gates in section 7 tell you early which scenario you are in.

## 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** *De laatste factuur uit Word* ("Your last invoice from Word")

**One-sentence summary:** A six-week Dutch launch campaign that persuades freelancers who still invoice from Word or Excel to send their next invoice with Tallyho, timed around the Q4 btw return deadline.

**Primary objective (SMART):** 1,500 paid Tallyho accounts from Dutch zzp'ers by 2027-03-31. At least 1,050 of them should be paying by the end of the six-week campaign (2027-02-22).

**Secondary objectives:**
- An activation rate of at least 60%, meaning trial users who send a real invoice within 72 hours of signing up.
- A trial-to-paid conversion rate of at least 35%.
- At least 30 active boekhouder partners by 2027-02-22.
- Learning which paid channel works. By the end of week 4, you should know your cost per paying customer on Google Search and on Meta, so that you have a benchmark for future campaigns.

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary segment: established zzp'ers who invoice from Word or Excel

> A Dutch freelancer in services (design, IT, consultancy, coaching, copywriting, photography and similar) who has been self-employed for one to five years. They send 2 to 15 invoices a month from a Word or Excel template they once built themselves. They struggle with keeping invoice numbers in order, chasing late payments, and adding up the VAT every quarter, and they want invoicing to take minutes instead of an evening. They discover tools through other freelancers, their boekhouder, LinkedIn and Google. They care most about price, simplicity and being "correct" towards the Belastingdienst.

### Secondary segment: new zzp'ers starting in January 2027

People who register at the KVK around the turn of the year and are about to send their first invoice. They have no habit to break yet. If you reach them before they copy a Word template from a friend, they are the easiest people to win.

### Influencer segment: boekhouders and small accounting firms

They are not buyers, but they strongly influence zzp clients. They benefit directly when clients hand in clean, digital, consistent invoices instead of a folder of Word files at tax time.

### Pain points and motivations

| Pain point | What they want instead |
|---|---|
| Copying last month's file, changing the number by hand, and worrying about duplicate or missing numbers | Numbering that is always correct without thinking about it |
| Not sure the invoice contains everything the Belastingdienst requires | Confidence that every invoice is correct |
| Clients paying late; awkward reminder emails | Reminders that go out automatically, and a payment link that makes paying easy |
| Adding up VAT from a pile of files every quarter | A btw total that is ready when the return is due |
| Looking less professional than larger competitors | A clean, branded invoice |
| Full bookkeeping packages feel expensive and complicated | One simple tool for invoicing at a predictable price |

**Main objection:** "Word is free and it works." The real competitor is doing nothing. The messaging must make switching feel like less effort than continuing.

### Where they spend time

- **Google:** searches such as "factuur maken", "factuur template word", "factuur programma zzp" and "wat moet er op een factuur".
- **LinkedIn:** freelancers in services are very active there in the Netherlands.
- **Facebook and Instagram:** zzp groups and niche groups (photographers, VAs, coaches), and Instagram for creative freelancers.
- **Associations and media:** ZZP Nederland, PZO, KVK content, Zipconomy, Sprout, and Dutch entrepreneur podcasts and newsletters. Check the reach and sponsorship rates of each before booking.
- **Their boekhouder,** especially in January (btw return) and March (income tax return).
- **Coworking spaces and local freelancer meetups.**

### Buying stage alignment

| Group | Stage | Job of the campaign |
|---|---|---|
| Waitlist (4,000) | Decision | Make them pay now, with a reason to act before 2027-01-31 |
| Word and Excel users searching for templates | Problem-aware, not looking for a solution | Show that the template is the problem |
| Freelancers comparing invoicing tools | Consideration | Win on simplicity and price clarity |
| New zzp'ers | Awareness | Make Tallyho the default first tool |

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** *Je volgende factuur in één minuut, en je btw-aangifte staat al klaar.* ("Your next invoice in one minute, and your VAT return is already prepared.")

**Message hierarchy:**
1. **Why should I care?** Invoicing from Word costs you an evening a month and still feels uncertain.
2. **What is the solution?** Tallyho turns invoicing into a one-minute task, with numbering, VAT and reminders handled for you.
3. **Why Tallyho?** It is built only for invoicing by Dutch zzp'ers, at one simple price of EUR 12 per month. It is not a full bookkeeping package you need to learn.
4. **What should I do?** Import your customers from Excel and send your next invoice free.

**Supporting messages and proof points:**

| Supporting message | Pain point addressed | Proof point (to confirm or produce) |
|---|---|---|
| "Always correct": every invoice contains what the Belastingdienst requires, with automatic sequential numbering | Uncertainty, numbering errors | Show the list of legal invoice requirements next to a Tallyho invoice. Get a quote from a boekhouder partner. |
| "Get paid faster": payment link and automatic reminders | Late payment | Beta data: average days to payment before and after. If you do not have this data yet, use a customer quote instead of a number. |
| "Your quarter, already added up": the btw overview is ready for the return | Quarterly VAT stress | Screenshot of the btw overview. Timed with the Q4 return deadline of 2027-01-31. |
| "Switch in ten minutes": import your customers from Excel and keep your existing numbering | Inertia ("Word works") | Timed onboarding video. Show the first invoice sent within 10 minutes. |

**Differentiation:** Against Word and Excel, Tallyho offers correctness, speed, reminders and the VAT overview. Against full bookkeeping suites, Tallyho is simpler and focused, and EUR 12 per month is a clear price. Do a competitor pricing check before you make any comparative claims. If you make claims, name features, not competitors.

**Tone by channel:**
- **Email to the waitlist:** personal, founder voice, "you were here first".
- **LinkedIn:** a founder's story and practical invoicing tips. Not salesy.
- **Search ads:** literal and benefit-led. They match the search query.
- **Meta and Instagram:** visual before-and-after (messy Word file versus a clean invoice), light humour.
- **Boekhouders:** professional. Focus on what it saves them: cleaner client administration and fewer questions.

**Trademark caution:** "Word" and "Excel" are Microsoft trademarks. Ad platforms may restrict their use in ad text. Keep the campaign name and the Word/Excel wording in owned and earned content (emails, blog, organic social, PR). In paid ads, say "je oude factuur-template" ("your old invoice template") unless the platform's policy allows the brand names.

### How the main channels work

**1. Waitlist and founding-member offer (the biggest lever).** Re-warm the list in December so the launch email does not land cold. On launch day, send a founding-member offer: **EUR 9 per month for the first 12 months**, or **EUR 99 for the first year**, valid for anyone who starts paying by 2027-01-31. Annual prepayment counts as paid right away and helps cash flow. Founding members can pay at once or take the 14-day trial; the offer price still applies when they convert before the deadline. Segment the list by how they engaged (opened December emails or not, beta or not), and send a final "price goes up tonight" email on 2027-01-31.

**2. Referrals.** Every paying customer gets a personal link. When the friend pays, both get one month free. Promote the link in three places: the screen after the first invoice is sent (the moment of highest satisfaction), the second onboarding email, and the founding-member welcome email.

**3. Boekhouders.** Start recruiting in December. The offer to partners:
- a free partner account with read access to client invoices (if the product supports it; if not, a CSV export guide);
- a 20% recurring commission for 12 months, paid from revenue and not from the campaign budget;
- a ready-to-send email to their zzp clients.

Target: 30 active partners by 2027-02-22, each referring 5 to 10 paying clients by the end of March. Income tax season (the return opens on 2027-03-01) is the second moment to activate partners.

**5. Factuurcheck and templates.** Build a free tool: upload or paste your Word invoice and see whether it contains every required element. Collect an email address to send the report, then move the person into a short trial sequence. Support it with SEO articles on "wat moet er op een factuur" and "factuur maken in Word". This channel builds up slowly. It contributes mostly in weeks 4 to 11, and the rankings are a long-term asset.

**6 to 8. Paid.** With no history, the first two weeks are for learning. Keep campaign structures simple, use few ad groups, and optimise for trial starts at first, because there will be too few paid conversions for the algorithms to learn from. Feed paid conversions back to the platforms as soon as the volume allows. Put about 63% of the paid budget into Google Search, the highest-confidence channel. Set up Consent Mode and server-side conversion tracking before launch, because cookie consent rules in the Netherlands will hide part of the conversions.

### Secondary KPIs

| KPI | Target | How it is tracked |
|---|---|---|
| Trial starts | about 4,000 in total (1,500 ÷ 0.38) | Product analytics, signup event with UTM source |
| Activation (first real invoice sent within 72 hours) | at least 60% | Product event `invoice_sent`, by cohort |
| Trial-to-paid conversion | at least 35% | Billing data joined to trial cohorts |
| Waitlist conversion to paid | at least 15% by 2027-02-22 | Email platform list matched against billing |
| Cost per paying customer, by paid channel | at most EUR 90 (Search), at most EUR 110 (Meta) | Ad platforms plus UTM attribution in billing; check by hand weekly |
| Blended cash CAC | at most EUR 10 | Total spend ÷ paid accounts |
| Referral share of new paid accounts | at least 15% | Referral tool |
| Active boekhouder partners | 30 by 2027-02-22 | Partner links with at least one referral |
| Month-1 churn among new payers | below 8% | Billing |

At EUR 12 per month, a blended CAC of EUR 10 pays back within the first month. A paid-channel cost of EUR 90 pays back in about 7.5 months. That is acceptable for learning, but not for scaling, unless retention is strong.

### Reporting cadence

- **Launch week:** a daily 15-minute check of signups, activation, payments, spend and errors.
- **Weekly:** a Monday report of cumulative paid accounts against target, results by channel, and next actions.

**Decision gates:**

- **Gate 1 (2027-01-25).** Is waitlist conversion to paid at least 8%, and are trial starts at least 1,200?
  - If not, add a second offer-led email, and have the founder send personal messages to people who engaged but did not convert.
  - Consider a stronger offer: two months free on the annual plan.
- **Gate 2 (2027-02-08).** Is the paid-channel cost per paying customer at most EUR 110?
  - If yes, move test money to the better channel.
  - If not, pause the weaker channel and move the budget to sponsored placements and partner activation.
- **Gate 3 (2027-02-22).** Are you at 1,050 paid accounts or more?
  - If you are below 900, decide whether to spend the contingency or reset the goal for March to a realistic number.
- **Final review (2027-04-07).** Results and learnings, and the benchmarks to use in future campaigns.

## 9. Risks and Mitigations
