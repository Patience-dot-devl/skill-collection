# Press release draft: CleanPlate Series A

**Assumptions made for this draft**

- The release goes out under an embargo that ends on a date to be confirmed. Placeholders in square brackets mark every fact that the brief did not give, such as dates, the founding year and contact details.
- "Cuts plastic by 60%" is read as 60% less plastic per box than CleanPlate's current packaging. The baseline and the method of measurement need confirmation before release.
- "120,000 active customers" is read as the current count of active subscribers in the Netherlands. Please confirm the definition of "active" (for example, at least one box in the last 90 days).
- The quotes from Ines de Wit and from Harbour Ventures are drafted for approval. Each speaker must approve their quote before publication.
- The release is written in English for international trade and tech press. A Dutch version for national media and a Dutch/French version for Belgian media are recommended.

## CleanPlate raises EUR 8 million Series A to expand to Belgium and cut plastic in its meal kits by 60%

*The Amsterdam meal-kit subscription, which has 120,000 active customers, closes a round led by Harbour Ventures with participation from Polder Capital*

**AMSTERDAM, [DATE]** – CleanPlate, the Amsterdam-based meal-kit subscription, today announced that it has raised EUR 8 million in Series A funding. Harbour Ventures led the round, and existing investor Polder Capital also took part. CleanPlate will use the new capital for two goals: to launch in Belgium in spring 2027, and to introduce a new packaging line that uses 60% less plastic than its current boxes.

CleanPlate delivers weekly boxes of pre-portioned ingredients and recipes to homes across the Netherlands. The company now serves 120,000 active customers. [Optional: add one growth figure, such as year-on-year customer growth or the number of meals delivered, if it can be published.]

### Expansion to Belgium

Belgium will be CleanPlate's first market outside the Netherlands. The company plans to start deliveries in spring 2027. **Notes for the editor before release**

1. Confirm the closing date of the round and the embargo date and time.
2. Confirm the plastic reduction baseline (per box, by weight, compared with which packaging) so that the 60% claim can be defended. Claims about environmental benefits are subject to consumer-protection rules in the Netherlands and the EU, so keep the evidence on file.
3. Confirm whether CleanPlate can publish the investor names and the round size together with the lead investor's consent.
4. Get written approval for every quote from the named speaker.
5. Choose one growth metric to add credibility, if the company is willing to publish it.
