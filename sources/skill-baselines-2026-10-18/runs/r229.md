# Brand check: draft-landing.md against the Tallyho brand guide

I did not change the draft file. The findings are below, followed by a suggested revision. File references use `draft-landing.md:line` for the copy and `guide:line` for `tallyho-brand-guide.md`.

## Must fix (rule breaks)

1. **Unprovable superlative.** `draft-landing.md:3` says Tallyho is "the best invoicing tool". The guide bans superlatives we cannot prove (`guide:16`). Replace it with a concrete benefit.
2. **Competitor named and disparaged.** `draft-landing.md:3` says "Unlike clunky Moneybird". The guide forbids naming or disparaging competitors in customer-facing copy (`guide:19`). Remove the comparison entirely.
3. **"Easy" and "just" about a task.** `draft-landing.md:6` uses "Easy setup" and "Just add your…". Both words are banned when describing a task (`guide:8`).
4. **Tax term casing.** `draft-landing.md:6` has "KvK-nummer" and `draft-landing.md:8` has "BTW". The guide requires lowercase "kvk-nummer" and "btw" (`guide:13`).
5. **Unsourced speed figure.** `draft-landing.md:7` claims clients "pay 2x faster". Any time-saving figure needs a footnoted source (`guide:17`). Add a footnote with the source, or drop the number if no source exists.
6. **Tax claim overreaches and lacks the required line.** `draft-landing.md:8` says filing "is guaranteed to be correct". That is a claim we cannot prove (`guide:16`), and any copy about tax filing must end with "Check with your accountant for your situation." (`guide:18`). Remove "guaranteed" and add the sentence.
7. **Two exclamation marks.** `draft-landing.md:10` ends with "!!". The limit is one per piece (`guide:7`), and a single full stop fits the "confident, not loud" voice better.
8. **Reader called a "customer".** `draft-landing.md:10` says "Join 5,000+ customers". The guide says never to call the reader "users" or "customers" (`guide:6`). Use "freelancers" or "zzp'ers" instead. The 5,000+ figure should also be verified before it goes live.

## Should fix (voice and messaging)

- **Passive, impersonal phrasing.** `draft-landing.md:6-8` use "are generated by the system", "are included" and "is provided". The guide asks for plain, direct copy that talks to the reader as "you" (`guide:6`). Rewrite these as active sentences addressed to the reader.
- **"customers" for the reader's own clients.** `draft-landing.md:7` uses "customers" for the people who pay the reader. That is technically allowed, but it sits next to line 10's misuse. "your clients" is clearer.
- **Implied dig in the headline.** `draft-landing.md:1`, "finally done right", implies that other tools get invoicing wrong. It is not a named attack, but it leans towards the disparagement that `guide:19` rules out. A benefit-led headline is safer.
- **Pillar 3 is underused.** The draft covers "Get paid faster" (line 7) and "Tax-ready all year" (line 8), but "Made for zzp'ers" (`guide:24`) appears only as "Dutch freelancers". iDEAL and Dutch-first are missing, and so is the term "zzp'ers".

## What already works

The product name is spelled "Tallyho" correctly at lines 3 and 10. The copy says "invoice", never "bill". Payment links on every invoice (line 7) and the quarterly tax overview (line 8) match pillars 1 and 2.
