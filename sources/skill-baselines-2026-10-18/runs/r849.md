## Notes on voice and tone

The release uses a neutral, factual news style. The lead paragraph answers who (CleanPlate), what (EUR 8M Series A), who led it (Harbour Ventures, with Polder Capital), where (Amsterdam, expanding to Belgium), when (announced today, Belgian launch in spring 2027) and why (expansion and plastic reduction). The CEO quote is written in a warmer, more human voice so that it adds a point of view rather than restating the facts.

## Before release: items to check

- **Verify the 60% claim.** Confirm the baseline (current packaging), the unit (per box, per meal or total volume) and how it was measured. Under EU and Dutch consumer-protection guidance on green claims, a sustainability claim should be specific and substantiated. Keep a short methodology note ready for journalists.
- **Confirm the Belgian launch scope.** Name the regions or cities if known, and decide whether the launch covers Flanders, Wallonia and Brussels. If French-speaking regions are included, consider a French and a Dutch version of the release.
- **Approve the quotes.** Ines de Wit should approve or rewrite her quote, and both investors should supply and approve their own quotes and boilerplates.
- **Check what can be disclosed.** Confirm with the investors whether the valuation, total funding to date and the size of each investor's share may be shared (the draft leaves these out).
- **Agree the timing.** Decide on an embargo time and date, and coordinate publication with Harbour Ventures and Polder Capital so that all parties announce at the same moment.
