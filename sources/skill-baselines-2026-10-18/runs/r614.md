# Competitive Brief: Tallyho versus Moneybird and e-Boekhouden

Research date: 2026-10-18. Competitor pricing was checked on the official pricing pages on the same day, with third-party comparison sites used only for context. Pricing changes often in this market, so re-check before any external use.

## Assumptions and a problem with the premise

I had to make some assumptions. First, I took the product facts in the request as true for your product: EUR 12 per month, invoicing for Dutch freelancers, iDEAL payment links on every invoice, a quarterly btw overview, and no bank-feed integration yet. I treated "Tallyho" as your product. Second, I assumed the EUR 12 figure is a monthly list price excluding VAT. Third, I could not confirm your product's details from public sources.

The public website at tallyho.app does not match this description. Its homepage describes "Beautifully simple time tracking for freelancers". It lists a free tier for up to 5 clients, gives no paid price, and does not mention invoicing, iDEAL, btw/VAT, or bank feeds. A search snippet from its blog also says the app deliberately leaves out built-in invoicing and lists a USD Pro plan. If your product is a different app with the same name, or the public site is out of date, the claims in this brief must be checked against your product before they go to sales. Competitive claims about "Tallyho" should not be published until that is resolved.

## 1. Executive summary

The competitive landscape splits by what the buyer needs today. Moneybird is the most direct rival for a freelancer who wants invoicing, iDEAL, and btw in one tool. Its bank feed exists, but it only appears on its higher tiers. e-Boekhouden is the most important price rival, because its ZZP entry plan is cheaper than EUR 12 on its listed price.

The biggest opportunity is to position on invoice-to-cash, not on price alone. Your product gets paid faster through iDEAL on every invoice and gives a btw overview without requiring a bookkeeping plan. The biggest threat is the bank-feed gap, plus the fact that "we are cheaper" is not true against e-Boekhouden's entry plan. Sales must not make that claim without a qualifier.

## 2. Competitor profiles

### Moneybird

- **Positioning:** Online bookkeeping and invoicing for small businesses and freelancers in the Netherlands. The official pricing page lists four plans.
- **Target buyer:** Small businesses, freelancers, and accountant-supported firms.
- **Pricing (official page, which appears to show annual-billing rates):** Compact EUR 3, Start EUR 15, Groei EUR 29, Compleet EUR 41 per month. Third-party sites quote monthly rates of EUR 4, 18, 35, and 49. I could not confirm the VAT basis on the page, so treat these as indicative.
- **Bank connection:** Compact and Start have no linked bank account; Start allows manual import with 20 transactions. Groei includes 5 linked accounts and 50 transactions. Compleet is unlimited. This matters because Moneybird's bank feed is a paid-tier feature, not a default.
- **iDEAL:** Online payment methods, including iDEAL, appear on all four plans, with a stated transaction cost of EUR 0.29. The page says customers can pay "via een online betaallink". I could not confirm that iDEAL links appear on every invoice by default.
- **VAT:** The page lists a VAT overview ("Btw-overzicht") on all plans. It does not say how often it runs, so "quarterly" is not confirmed. Electronic VAT returns are included from Start upward.
- **Strengths:** A mature product, a broad feature set, a large Dutch user base, and bank connection on higher tiers.
- **Weaknesses:** Entry tiers have no bank feed, and transaction caps push freelancers up a tier. Several third-party sources show older prices, which suggests the plan structure has changed recently. That is a source of confusion for buyers.

### e-Boekhouden.nl

- **Positioning:** A Dutch bookkeeping package with a ZZP (self-employed) entry plan.
- **Pricing (official page, checked the same day):** ZZP Pakket EUR 9.95 per month, or EUR 4.98 in the first 12 months. Standaard EUR 14.50, with no invoicing. Standaard plus Factureren EUR 24.00, with unlimited bookings and invoicing. Starters can get up to 15 months free. The page does not say whether these prices include VAT.
- **Limits:** The ZZP Pakket has unlimited invoicing but caps bookkeeping at 240 bookings per fiscal year.
- **Bank connection:** The official page lists bank import (Bankkoppeling) as a feature but does not state which plans include it. One third-party source says it is in every package. Treat this as unconfirmed.
- **iDEAL:** Listed among online payment options ("Betalen via iDEAL | Wero, Creditcard, PayPal etc"). Plan inclusion is not stated.
- **Strengths:** The lowest entry price in this set, a long starter offer, and a mature bookkeeping core.
- **Weaknesses:** The booking cap on the cheapest plan, and the step up to EUR 24 for invoicing on a full plan.

### Tallyho (your product, as described in the request)

- **Positioning, as given:** Invoicing for Dutch freelancers, with iDEAL on every invoice, a quarterly btw overview, and no bank feed yet.
- **Price, as given:** EUR 12 per month.
- **Public evidence:** None found. See the premise section above.

## 4. Content gap analysis

I did not audit competitor blogs, social channels, or review sites for this brief. The sources I could access were the pricing pages and a small number of third-party comparisons. That gap should be closed before any content plan is set. The gaps I can see from pricing and feature pages are these. Moneybird and e-Boekhouden both explain their plan structure in detail, while your product's pages (as far as I can tell) do not. A plain comparison page that explains what each plan includes, with the bank-feed trade-off stated openly, would address a real search need.

## 5. Opportunities

1. **Invoice-to-cash is an unclaimed angle.** The clearest message is that a freelancer gets paid through iDEAL on every invoice and does not have to buy a bank-feed tier to do it. Neither competitor leads with this on its main pages.
2. **Undercut Moneybird's entry tiers on value.** Moneybird's Start plan costs EUR 15 to EUR 18 for invoicing with manual bank import. EUR 12 with iDEAL and a btw overview is a credible comparison there.
3. **Target the buyer who does not need bank matching yet.** New freelancers who reconcile by hand, or who hand their books to an accountant, do not need a bank feed on day one. Lead with that segment.
4. **Be honest about bank feed, and turn the gap into a timing message.** A roadmap date, if you have one, is the strongest form of this message. Without a date, say what buyers can do today.

## 6. Threats

1. **The "cheaper" claim does not hold against e-Boekhouden's ZZP Pakket.** EUR 9.95 list and EUR 4.98 for a year both undercut EUR 12. Against Moneybird's Start tier, you are cheaper. Against e-Boekhouden's full invoicing plan at EUR 24, you are cheaper by a wide margin. The claim should be made only in those comparisons.
2. **Bank feed is a real gap for buyers who need automatic matching.** Moneybird offers it from EUR 29 to EUR 35 on Groei. A buyer who needs matching will pay more to Moneybird than your price, but they will get a feature you do not have.
3. **Price confusion in the market.** Moneybird's pricing has changed several times in 2026, and third-party sites quote different numbers. Buyers may compare against old prices, so sales should quote the current official page and say the date.
4. **Premise risk.** If the public "Tallyho" brand is a different, time-tracking product, prospects who search for it may find a mismatched offer. Brand clarity is a threat until it is fixed.

## 7. Recommended actions

**Positioning recommendation.** Do not position as "the cheapest". Position as "get paid faster from your invoices, without paying for a bank feed you do not need yet". Target freelancers who invoice clients in the Netherlands and want iDEAL and a btw overview. Make the bank-feed gap a stated, planned trade-off, not something to hide.

**What sales should say (suggested wording, subject to the premise check):**

- Opening: "If you invoice clients and want to be paid through iDEAL without a bookkeeping package, we are built for that at EUR 12 a month."
- On price against e-Boekhouden: "e-Boekhouden's ZZP plan is cheaper for bookkeeping, but its invoicing needs the EUR 24 plan. If you need invoicing and iDEAL, the comparison is us at EUR 12 against them at EUR 24."
- On price against Moneybird: "Moneybird's Start plan gives you invoicing without a bank feed for EUR 15 or more. Our EUR 12 gives you iDEAL and a btw overview. If you need automatic bank matching today, Moneybird's Groei plan is the better fit, and we will say so."
- On the bank feed: "We do not have a bank feed yet. Here is how you get your transactions in today: [insert your current workaround]. [Insert roadmap date, if one is committed.]" Do not claim a feature that does not exist.

**Quick wins this week:**

1. Confirm the premise: whether your product is the one described, and whether the public tallyho.app site is current. Fix any mismatch before sales uses this brief.
2. Confirm whether your EUR 12 is monthly or annual, and whether it excludes VAT. Sales must quote the same basis as the competitor figures.
3. Write the bank-feed workaround in one sentence, so sales has an answer when a prospect asks.
4. Re-check the Moneybird and e-Boekhouden pricing pages before each sales call, because both have changed in 2026.

**Strategic moves:**

1. Build a comparison page with plain, dated price tables for the three products, with the bank-feed trade-off stated openly.
2. Decide whether a bank feed is on the roadmap and by when. A committed date changes the objection from "you do not have it" to "you will have it".
3. Track win and loss reasons against Moneybird and e-Boekhouden, and check which objection responses work.
