# Competitive brief: Tallyho versus Moneybird and e-Boekhouden

Prepared 2026-10-18. Competitor prices and features were checked on the vendors' own pricing pages on that date. Neither page says whether its prices include or exclude btw, so all prices below are compared as listed.

## Assumptions

- Tallyho costs EUR 12 per month, billed monthly, with unlimited invoices. It has no annual discount and no starter offer.
- The quarterly btw overview helps the freelancer prepare the aangifte, but Tallyho does not file the return with the Belastingdienst.
- Tallyho has no bank feed and no bank statement import, so payments are matched only through the iDEAL links it creates itself.
- The buyer is a Dutch freelancer (zzp'er) with a fairly small number of clients and invoices, who may or may not have an accountant.

## The short answer

Do not position Tallyho as the cheapest option, because it is not. Position it as **the simplest way for a freelancer to send an invoice and get paid**: every invoice carries an iDEAL link, and the btw for the quarter is already added up. Tallyho is an invoicing and getting-paid tool, not bookkeeping software. Framed that way, the missing bank feed becomes a question of scope ("we don't do bookkeeping") instead of a missing feature ("we can't do bookkeeping yet").

Sell against **Moneybird Start** and against freelancers who are leaving free plans. Do not take on Moneybird Groei, or e-Boekhouden's full bookkeeping packages, with buyers who want automatic bank reconciliation.

## What the competitors actually offer

### Moneybird (moneybird.nl/prijzen)

| Plan | Price per month (monthly billing) | Link an external bank account | Bank transactions processed per month | Sales invoices | Electronic btw return |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compact | EUR 3 | No | 5 | 1 | No |
| Start | EUR 15 | No | 20 | Unlimited | Yes |
| Groei | EUR 29 | 5 accounts | 50 | Unlimited | Yes |
| Compleet | EUR 41 | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Yes |

- Every plan includes online payment links (iDEAL, SEPA direct debit, card, Apple Pay and Google Pay, Bancontact). iDEAL costs EUR 0.29 per transaction.
- Paying yearly saves EUR 36 on Start. That brings Start to EUR 144 a year, which is **EUR 12 per month, the same as Tallyho**.
- Start lets you import bank transactions by hand and caps the number you can process at 20 per month. Unused transactions do not carry over to the next month.
- There is a 60-day free trial of Compleet. Moneybird dropped its free plan on 1 July 2026 (reported by nieuws.nl).

**What this means:** Moneybird Start, the plan a freelancer would actually compare with Tallyho, has **no bank feed either**. Customers only get an automatic bank feed at EUR 29 per month. Against Moneybird, Tallyho's real price advantage is EUR 3 a month on monthly billing and nothing on yearly billing. Moneybird Start also files the btw return electronically, and Tallyho does not.

### e-Boekhouden.nl (e-boekhouden.nl/prijzen)

| Package | First 12 months | Regular price | Bookings | Invoicing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ZZP | EUR 4.98 per month | EUR 9.95 per month | Up to 240 per financial year | Unlimited |
| Standaard | EUR 7.25 per month | EUR 14.50 per month | Unlimited | Not included |
| Standaard + Factureren | EUR 12.00 per month | EUR 24.00 per month | Unlimited | Unlimited |

- New businesses get 15 months free from the date they register with the KvK, with all features, including bank connections.
- New customers who are not starters get 50% off for 12 months.
- The site lists iDEAL (with Wero), credit card and PayPal payments on invoices, and automatic bank connections. The pricing page does not say clearly which features each package includes. Sales should check this before claiming anything specific.

**What this means:** e-Boekhouden is **cheaper than Tallyho** at every stage. The ZZP package costs EUR 9.95 at the regular price, half that in the first year, and nothing at all for 15 months for a new business. It is full bookkeeping software with a bank connection. Tallyho cannot win against it on price or on features. It can only win on simplicity and on how the product feels to use.

### Market timing

Free plans are disappearing in this segment. Moneybird ended its free plan on 1 July 2026, and Rompslomp will charge EUR 1.50 per invoice sent on its free Starter plan from 1 November 2026 (nieuws.nl). This produces a group of price-aware freelancers who are looking for a new tool **right now**. That is Tallyho's best near-term target.

## Where to position

**Category:** "Invoicing and getting paid for freelancers", not "bookkeeping".

**Positioning statement:** For Dutch freelancers who want to send an invoice and be paid the same day without learning bookkeeping software, Tallyho puts an iDEAL payment link on every invoice and keeps a running btw total for each quarter, for a flat EUR 12 a month. Moneybird and e-Boekhouden are built for keeping the books. Tallyho is built for getting paid.

**Best-fit customers (go after these):**
1. Freelancers who leave the bookkeeping to an accountant and only need to invoice, get paid and hand over a clean quarterly btw figure.
2. Freelancers with a low volume of invoices and a few clients who find Moneybird or e-Boekhouden too much software for what they need.
3. People who have just lost a free plan (former Moneybird free users, and Rompslomp Starter users facing per-invoice fees). For anyone sending more than about 8 invoices a month, Tallyho's flat EUR 12 is cheaper than Rompslomp at EUR 1.50 per invoice.

**Poor-fit customers (do not chase these):**
- Freelancers who want automatic bank reconciliation, expense booking and a full ledger. Send them to Moneybird Groei or e-Boekhouden honestly. A customer who churns after two months costs more than a lost deal.
- Brand-new businesses that qualify for e-Boekhouden's 15 months free, if price is their main reason for choosing.
- Freelancers who want the software to file the btw return for them.

## What sales should say

### Opening pitch (about 30 seconds)

"Tallyho is made for one job: getting you paid. Every invoice you send has an iDEAL button on it, so your client can pay in a few seconds from their phone. You can see straight away who has paid. At the end of each quarter your btw is already added up, ready for your return or for your accountant. There is no bookkeeping software to learn. It costs a flat twelve euros a month."

### Key messages, in order

1. **Paid faster.** An iDEAL link on every invoice is standard, not an add-on. Lead with this, because it is the benefit the customer feels most.
2. **Btw is never a surprise.** The quarterly overview shows what you owe before the deadline arrives.
3. **Simple on purpose.** Tallyho does invoicing and getting paid, and does nothing else. Setup takes minutes, not an afternoon.
4. **A flat, predictable price.** EUR 12 a month with no invoice limits and no transaction caps. (Check what Tallyho actually charges per iDEAL payment before comparing it with Moneybird's EUR 0.29.)

### Objection handling

**"You don't have a bank connection."**
"That's right, and it's deliberate for now. Tallyho knows an invoice is paid because the payment goes through its own iDEAL link, so you don't need a bank feed to see who has paid you. If you want every bank transaction booked automatically, that is a bookkeeping job. For comparison, Moneybird's Start plan doesn't include a bank connection either. You need their EUR 29 Groei plan for that." Only add "a bank connection is on our roadmap" if product has committed to a date. Do not promise it otherwise.

**"e-Boekhouden is cheaper, or free for starters."**
"It is, and if you want full bookkeeping software, it's a good choice. Tallyho is for people who don't want to do bookkeeping and just want to invoice and get paid. Many of our customers tried a full package and found they only used the invoice part." Do not argue about price here, because Tallyho loses that argument.

**"Moneybird is the standard, and my accountant knows it."**
"Moneybird is a good product. On their Start plan you pay EUR 15 a month on monthly billing and can process 20 bank transactions a month, and you still don't get a bank connection. Tallyho is twelve euros, has no limits, and gives you the quarterly btw figures your accountant needs." If export to the accountant (CSV, PDF, or a link to the accountant's software) is weak, fix it before you use this line.

**"Can Tallyho file my btw return?"**
"Tallyho prepares the figures, and you or your accountant submit them in Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk. It takes a few minutes, because the totals are already there." Be honest here. Moneybird Start does file the return electronically.

### What sales must not say

- "We're the cheapest." This is false compared with e-Boekhouden ZZP and with annual billing on Moneybird Start.
- "You don't need a bank feed." Some customers do need one. Say instead that Tallyho is not the right tool for them.
- Any claim about specific e-Boekhouden package contents that you have not checked with them, because their pricing page is unclear.
- Anything negative about the competitors. Compare scope instead.

## Recommendations for product and marketing

1. **Introduce annual billing.** At the moment, Moneybird Start billed yearly costs the same EUR 12 a month as Tallyho. An annual plan at about EUR 10 a month (EUR 120 a year) would bring back a clear price difference.
2. **Add a CSV or MT940 bank statement import** as a stopgap before a full bank feed. That removes most of the force of the bank-feed objection at low build cost.
3. **Build a switching campaign** aimed at former Moneybird free users and Rompslomp Starter users, timed for before 1 November 2026. Include a free first month and a simple way to import existing clients.
4. **Publish the price of an iDEAL payment.** If it is below Moneybird's EUR 0.29, it becomes a selling point. If it is above, sales needs a prepared answer.
5. **Create an accountant-friendly export** (a quarterly btw summary plus an invoice list), because accountants often decide which tool a freelancer uses.
