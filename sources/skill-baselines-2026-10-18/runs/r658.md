# Competitive brief: Tallyho versus Moneybird and e-Boekhouden

Date: 2026-10-18

## Assumptions and verification status

Before the positioning advice, the limits of this brief need to be stated clearly.

1. Tallyho's product facts are taken from the request, not verified. The request says Tallyho costs EUR 12 per month, is an invoicing app for Dutch freelancers, offers iDEAL payment links on every invoice, and provides a quarterly btw overview. A web search for a Dutch invoicing product with those features returned nothing. The only "TallyHo" listings found describe an unrelated time-tracking tool for solo workers, sold at up to about USD 5 per month. Before any sales material is published, someone must confirm the price (including or excluding VAT), the iDEAL behaviour, the btw overview, and the bank-feed status against Tallyho's own website.
2. Competitor prices come from vendor pages fetched on 2026-10-18. Neither vendor page says clearly whether prices include or exclude VAT. I have treated all prices as excluding VAT, which is the usual Dutch B2B convention, but this should be checked. If the EUR 12 figure for Tallyho includes VAT, the comparison changes.
3. The brief assumes the company is a solo Dutch freelancer or small sole trader (zzp'er) audience, because the request names that segment.
4. Some third-party reviews were found. One Trustpilot review (dated 2026-07-08) claims that Moneybird dropped bank connection from its cheapest plan. This is one person's account and is not confirmed by Moneybird's own price page, so it is treated as unverified.

## What the competitors charge and include

**Moneybird** (fetched from moneybird.nl/prijzen, monthly billing):

- Compact: EUR 3 per month (EUR 24 per year). Per the fetched page, no bank connection, but iDEAL payment links are included. Limits on this plan were not confirmed.
- Start: EUR 15 per month (EUR 144 per year). Bank transactions can only be imported manually, with no automatic bank connection. iDEAL payment links are included.
- Groei: EUR 29 per month (EUR 276 per year). Automatic bank connection for up to 5 accounts. iDEAL payment links are included.
- Compleet: EUR 41 per month (EUR 396 per year). Unlimited bank connections. iDEAL payment links are included.
- iDEAL costs EUR 0.29 per transaction on top of the subscription.
- The page lists a btw overview on all plans, but does not say whether it is quarterly.

**e-Boekhouden** (fetched from e-boekhouden.nl/prijzen):

- ZZP: EUR 9.95 per month (EUR 4.98 per month in the first 12 months). Bookings are capped at 240 per year. Invoicing is included.
- Standaard: EUR 14.50 per month. Unlimited bookings, but no invoicing ("zonder factureren").
- Standaard + Factureren: EUR 24.00 per month. Unlimited bookings and unlimited invoicing. iDEAL and other online payment methods are listed under invoicing.
- The page lists automatic bank links as a feature, but the comparison does not show which plans include them. Bank-link manuals on the e-Boekhouden CDN say a subscription and a business bank account are needed, and list automatic links with ING, Rabobank, ABN AMRO, Knab, bunq and Triodos, among others.
- A plan can have a separate bank fee on top. ABN AMRO's January 2026 tariff sheet lists a bookkeeping-link fee of EUR 3.25 per account per month for the first account (excluding VAT).

## What this means for "we are cheaper"

The price claim is weaker than the brief assumes.

- Against Moneybird, EUR 12 is cheaper than Groei (EUR 29) and Start (EUR 15), but more expensive than Compact (EUR 3), which also has iDEAL links. Compact's limits must be checked before any claim is made against it.
- Against e-Boekhouden, EUR 12 is more expensive than the ZZP plan at EUR 9.95, which also includes invoicing. The only way to be clearly cheaper than e-Boekhouden on a like-for-like basis is to compare with its Standaard + Factureren plan (EUR 24), which is a bigger product.
- Price therefore cannot carry the positioning alone. Sales should not say "we are cheaper than everyone" unless the company confirms its own price, including VAT, and checks the competitor prices again on the day of use.

## Where the bank-feed gap matters

Automatic bank feeds are the main feature gap. Moneybird reserves them for its EUR 29 and EUR 41 plans, and e-Boekhouden offers them alongside its bookkeeping. A customer who wants receipts matched to bank lines automatically will find Tallyho lacking, and at the moment the company cannot close that gap. Some customers do not need bank matching, though. Many solo freelancers mainly want to send invoices, get paid through iDEAL, and file btw without learning a bookkeeping system. Those customers are the segment where Tallyho can compete honestly.

## Recommended positioning

Position Tallyho as the simplest way for a Dutch solo freelancer to send an invoice, get paid by iDEAL, and see what btw is owed each quarter. Do not position it as a full bookkeeping suite. The message is "invoice to paid, with the btw already counted", not "cheaper than your accountant software".

This positioning has three advantages. It avoids a fight on the feature where the company is weakest. It uses the iDEAL-on-every-invoice and quarterly-btw features, which are central to the request and are the reason a freelancer would switch. And it leaves the price as a supporting point rather than the lead.

The positioning should be revisited when bank feeds are on the roadmap with a date. Until then, sales should not imply that bank matching exists or is coming soon without a confirmed date.

## What sales should say

These are talk-track lines, not promises. Each one assumes the facts are confirmed as noted above.

- Opening: "If you send invoices and want to get paid through iDEAL without setting up a bookkeeping system, Tallyho does that for EUR 12 per month. The btw overview for each quarter is included."
- When the prospect asks about bank feeds: "No, Tallyho does not connect to your bank yet. If automatic bank matching is essential to you, a tool like Moneybird's Groei plan or e-Boekhouden will fit better today. If you mainly need invoicing, iDEAL payments and btw, we are built for that." Do not claim a manual import feature unless the product has one.
- When the prospect compares with Moneybird: "Moneybird's Compact plan is cheaper, but it has fewer features. Its bank connection is only on the Groei and Compleet plans, which cost EUR 29 and EUR 41 per month. Tallyho is in the middle on price and does not have bank matching yet." Check the Compact limits before using this line.
- When the prospect compares with e-Boekhouden: "e-Boekhouden's ZZP plan is cheaper, but it caps bookings at 240 per year. Tallyho is built around invoices and btw rather than full bookkeeping." Confirm the cap is still current before using it.
- When the prospect asks about VAT on the price: state clearly whether the EUR 12 includes or excludes VAT, and never let a prospect assume a different basis.

Sales should avoid these phrases: "best in the market", "cheaper than all alternatives", and "full bookkeeping". Each one is either unverified or contradicted by the competitor data above.

## Open items before this brief is used

1. Confirm Tallyho's price, VAT basis, iDEAL behaviour and quarterly btw overview from Tallyho's own product pages.
2. Confirm whether Moneybird Compact has usable invoicing limits and whether its iDEAL links work the same as on higher plans.
3. Confirm which e-Boekhouden plans include automatic bank links, and whether the bank's own fee applies.
4. Decide whether a bank-feed date can be shared with prospects, and if not, keep the bank-feed line in the talk track as written.
