# Brand review: Tallyho launch email

Reviewed: <eval-root>/inputs/draft-email.md
Against: <eval-root>/inputs/tallyho-brand-guide.md
Date: 2026-10-18

## Verdict

The draft should not ship as written. It breaks the terminology rules (product name, "bill", "btw", "users"), uses a banned word ("Simply"), and makes three claims that the guide does not support or that need a source or a disclaimer: the "#1" superlative, the time-saving figures, and the automatic BTW filing with its penalty promise. The fixes are mostly wording changes. The one substantive question is whether Tallyho really files the BTW return, because the guide describes a btw overview per quarter, not filing. Someone on the product side needs to confirm that before any copy goes out.

I checked the draft against the guide only. The guide is an excerpt, so other rules may apply. I did not run a web search, because every issue here is an internal rule or a missing source, not a current fact. I also could not verify the product facts (bank connection, free trial, automatic filing, the 30-second and 10-hour figures) because the materials contain no sources for them.

## Findings

### Must fix (rule violations)

1. Subject line, draft-email.md:1. "TallyHo" should be "Tallyho" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:11, product name is always "Tallyho"). The subject also claims "the #1 invoicing app for freelancers". That is an unprovable superlative (tallyho-brand-guide.md:16). Replace it with a claim you can support.

2. Body, draft-email.md:5. "Simply connect your bank" uses "simply", which the guide bans for describing a task (tallyho-brand-guide.md:8). "for users like you" breaks the rule against "users" in copy addressed to the reader (tallyho-brand-guide.md:6). Address the reader as "you" and "freelancers".

3. Body, draft-email.md:5 and :8. "a bill from Word" and "a professional bill" use "bill". The guide requires "invoice" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:12). Both occurrences should say "invoice".

4. Body, draft-email.md:10. "BTW" should be lowercase "btw" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:13).

5. Body, draft-email.md:10. The sentence about tax filing and late penalties does not end with the required line "Check with your accountant for your situation." (tallyho-brand-guide.md:18). The guide also says any tax filing or penalty copy must end that way. The sentence "so you'll never pay a late penalty again" is also an absolute promise the product cannot back up. Remove it.

6. Body, draft-email.md:12. "Our users save 10 hours every month" uses "users" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:6) and states a time-saving figure with no footnote source (tallyho-brand-guide.md:17). Remove it unless you have a source you can footnote.

7. Body, draft-email.md:8. "Send a professional bill in 30 seconds" states a time figure with no source (tallyho-brand-guide.md:17). It also uses "bill" (see item 3).

8. Sign-off, draft-email.md:14. "The TallyHo team" should be "The Tallyho team" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:11).

### Needs a decision before shipping (claims that may be false)

9. Draft-email.md:10. "Let TallyHo file your BTW return automatically" is the most serious issue. Pillar 2 describes a btw overview per quarter (tallyho-brand-guide.md:23), not filing. If Tallyho does not file returns, this is a false product claim. If it does, the pillar text is out of date and the product team should confirm the wording. I have written the revision below to describe the overview, which is what the guide states.

10. Draft-email.md:9 and :12. "Accept iDEAL payments" is consistent with pillar 3 (tallyho-brand-guide.md:24). "Start your free trial today" is not covered by the guide. Confirm that a free trial exists and is still offered at launch.

11. Draft-email.md:5. "Simply connect your bank and you're ready" makes a setup claim. Confirm that bank connection is required and works as described.

### Missing pillar coverage (not a violation, but weakens the email)

12. Pillar 1 (tallyho-brand-guide.md:22) is "Get paid faster (payment links on every invoice)". The draft mentions iDEAL but not payment links. Add a bullet that says each invoice carries a payment link.

13. Pillar 3 (tallyho-brand-guide.md:24) includes "kvk-nummer on every invoice". The draft does not mention it. Add it, because it is the clearest sign that the product is made for zzp'ers.

14. Pillar 2 is only half used, and the draft gets it wrong (see item 9). The corrected wording below uses the guide's own description.

### Checked and fine

- Exclamation marks: one, in the subject (draft-email.md:1). The guide allows at most one per piece (tallyho-brand-guide.md:7).
- Competitors: none named. "a bill from Word" describes a habit, not a competitor, so it does not break tallyho-brand-guide.md:19. I left it in the revision because it reads naturally, but you could drop it if legal prefers.
- Tone: the rest of the copy is plain and direct, and it addresses the reader as "you" (tallyho-brand-guide.md:6), apart from the "users" lines above.

## Suggested revision

Subject: Tallyho is here: invoicing built for zzp'ers

Hi there,

We built Tallyho for freelancers like you who are tired of sending invoices from Word. Connect your bank to get started.

With Tallyho you can:
- Send professional invoices, each with your kvk-nummer on it
- Add a payment link to every invoice and accept iDEAL payments
- Keep an overview of your btw per quarter

Check with your accountant for your situation.

Start your free trial today. [Confirm the trial exists before sending.]

The Tallyho team

Notes on the revision:
- The 30-second and 10-hour figures are removed. Reinstate them only with a source you can footnote (tallyho-brand-guide.md:17).
- "Connect your bank" keeps the original setup claim. Confirm it (finding 11).
- The btw line describes the overview the guide names, not filing. If Tallyho files returns, the product team must confirm that and the copy can say so, with the accountant line kept at the end of the tax content (tallyho-brand-guide.md:18).
- The revision has no exclamation marks, which is within the limit.

## Open questions for the team

- Does Tallyho file the BTW return, or only provide the quarterly overview? This decides the wording of the tax bullet.
- Is there a source for the 30-second and 10-hour figures, and for the claim that Tallyho is the number one invoicing app? If not, the claims stay out.
- Does the free trial exist at launch?
