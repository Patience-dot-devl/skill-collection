# Inbox

Drop uncurated material here: a skill folder you are evaluating, an export, a link list, notes.

Nothing in `_inbox/` is referenced from other documents until it is filed. The check script skips
this folder. To file an item:

1. Decide what it is. A skill to vendor goes under `skills/<plugin>/skills/`. A raw export or
   evidence goes to `sources/`. A decision or analysis becomes a document in `docs/`.
2. Register any live source it came from in
   [`foundation/data-sources.md`](../foundation/data-sources.md).
3. Delete it from `_inbox/` once filed.
